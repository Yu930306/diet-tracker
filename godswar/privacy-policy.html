Privacy Policy — Gods War (众神之战)

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Contact: 287859172@qq.com

1. What this game is

Gods War is a 1v1 ranked card battle game set in Greek mythology. In the online version you play ranked matches against real players, join 4-player private rooms with real friends, and keep your progress on your account. A match takes 3–7 minutes.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the game needs to run online play:

  • Account information. When you create an account: an account identifier and the display name you choose. We do not ask for your real name, phone number, or home address to play. If you sign in with TapTap Login, TapTap handles the sign-in; we receive only the account identifier needed to recognise you.
  • Gameplay data. Your rank and stars, match history and results, unlocked gods, decks, season progress, and in-game currency (god dust) balances. This is what lets your progress travel with your account and lets matchmaking pair you fairly.
  • Device and connection data. Device model, operating system version, game version, and IP address — used to connect you to a match, keep the connection stable, support reconnect after a drop, and protect the service against abuse.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No location data. We do not request or use your precise location.
  • No contacts, photos, or files. The game does not access them.
  • No payment details. Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases, and we do not collect any payment information.
  • No chat content. There is no in-game chat and no player-to-player messaging, so there is no message content to collect.
  • No advertising data. The game contains no advertising SDK and no third-party ad tracker.

4. How we use your data

To run matches, matchmaking, ranks, private rooms, and reconnect; to save and restore your progress on your account; to keep the service secure and fair (detecting abuse, cheating, or service attacks); and to answer your support messages. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Sharing and storage

Matchmaking and account data are processed on servers operated by the developer (Guangzhou Sanyi Garment Co., Ltd., Guangzhou, China). They are processed only to run the game, under our instructions. If you sign in with TapTap Login, TapTap processes the sign-in under TapTap's own terms and privacy policy. We disclose data only when required by applicable law. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Account and gameplay data are kept while your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards to restore accounts and resolve disputes.

Delete your account: email 287859172@qq.com from the email tied to your account and ask for deletion. We will delete your account data and confirm by reply. Deleting the app from your device removes local data only; it does not delete your account. Use the email above to delete the account itself.

7. Children

The game is rated 12+. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has created an account, email 287859172@qq.com and we will delete it.

8. AI-generated content

  • God portraits, key art, and other game artwork are AI-assisted original artwork created for this game. They do not copy another artist's work.
  • Sound effects and music are procedurally synthesised (generated by software, not recorded from real performers); they contain no cloned human voice.
  • The game has no AI chat and no AI-generated user content.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what we collect — for example adding chat, analytics, advertising, or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the new version will ask you to review the change. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.
